Spicy Tonkotsu Ramen

Description

A natural tonkotsu soup that removes any unwanted off-flavors and brings out the maximum deliciousness of tonkotsu.

These special raw noodles are made from a unique blend of quality flours, with the foremost consideration being synergy with our soup. Furthermore, the original "secret red sauce," made by blending and aging dozens of ingredients with chili peppers as the base, adds even more depth to the flavor.

Ingredients

  • Secret red sauce
  • Dashi
  • Classic tonkotsu broth
  • Fresh noodles

Steps

  1. Simmer the Broth: Combine the 300 ml water, pork belly, lard, sake, garlic powder, pepper, and gelatin in a small pot. Bring to a boil, cover, and simmer for 10 minutes.
  2. Blend and Season: Blend the soup with a handheld blender for about 1 minute. Add the soy sauce, sugar, salt, and soy milk, then blend for another 10 seconds.
  3. Make the Spicy Sauce: Mix your gochujang, shichimi togarashi, pepper, sesame oil, and water in a small bowl until it forms a smooth paste.
  4. Boil Noodles & Assemble: Boil your thin straight noodles in a separate pot to your preferred firmness (about 2 minutes), drain, and place into a bowl. Pour the hot creamy soup over the noodles, top with green onions, chashu, and add the spicy red sauce in the center.
