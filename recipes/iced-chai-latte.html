Iced Chai Latte

Description

This iced chai latte is a cool, sweet, and spiced tea drink made by combining black tea concentrate, milk, spice and ice.

Ingredients

  • Masala chai adagio
  • Chai
  • Hot water
  • Sugar
  • Cinnamon
  • Whipped cream

Steps

  1. Steep the black tea bags in 1 cup of boiling water for about 5 minutes, then remove and discard the tea bags. 2. Make.
  2. In a jar, combine the maple syrup and spices (cinnamon, ginger, cloves, and cardamom). Pour the hot brewed tea into the jar and stir well until combined.
  3. Let the concentrate cool. You can store it in the refrigerator for up to 5 days.
  4. Fill a glass with 1 ½ cups of ice. Pour in your desired amount of chai concentrate (about 6 to 8 tablespoons), top with ¾ cup of milk, stir well.
